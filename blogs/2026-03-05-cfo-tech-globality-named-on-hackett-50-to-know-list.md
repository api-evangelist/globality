Globality has been named to The Hackett Group's 2025-2026 "50 to Know" list, which tracks procurement technology providers identified by the advisory firm's Solution Intelligence analysts. The list is part of The Hackett Group's annual review of the global procurement technology market. Globality said it was the only "autonomous sourcing pure-play specialist" included.
